fethr

Light as a feather, free as your speech.

fethr is a tray app for Windows that turns any key into a push-to-talk dictation button. Hold it, speak, let go — the transcript is pasted straight into whatever window has focus. Speech recognition runs on your own whisper.cpp server and the optional cleanup pass on your own Ollama instance, so nothing you say leaves the machines you control. A companion USB macro-pad, the sidecar, gives you dedicated keys, joysticks and an LED panel, configured from the Sidecar page.
